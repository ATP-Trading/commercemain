This policy explains how ATP Trading (“we”, “us”) handles personal information when you use www.atpgroupservices.ae, checkout.atpgroupservices.ae, our customer account services, or contact us about an order. It applies to visitors, customers and ATP members.

Information we collect

  • Contact and order details you provide, such as your name, email, phone number, billing and delivery addresses, items ordered, payment confirmation and return requests.
  • Account and membership information needed to sign you in, manage membership status and provide benefits.
  • Messages, reviews and other information you choose to send us. Please do not send payment card details or sensitive information through ordinary email or WhatsApp.
  • Technical and usage information, such as IP address, browser and device information, pages visited, product interactions, shopping cart activity and cookie preferences.
  • Information from service providers supporting payments, delivery, account access and store operations, including transaction status and delivery updates.

Payment providers collect the payment information needed for the payment method you select. If you choose Tabby, Tamara or another external provider, that provider also processes your information under its own terms and privacy notice.

How we use information

We use information to process and deliver orders, manage payments and returns, operate customer accounts and membership benefits, respond to enquiries, maintain security, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. Optional analytics help us understand and improve the store. Where you consent or applicable law otherwise permits, we use information for marketing and advertising.

We rely on the grounds permitted by applicable law, including your consent where required, processing necessary to provide a service you request or perform a contract, and compliance with legal obligations. Reading this policy or visiting the website does not by itself grant consent to optional analytics or advertising cookies.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

Essential cookies and similar storage support functions such as the cart, account security and remembering your privacy choices. On our storefront, optional analytics use Google Analytics and Shopify to understand site usage. Optional advertising technologies use Google and Meta to measure and personalise advertising.

The consent panel lets you choose “Allow all”, “Analytics only” or “Decline”. You can reopen “Analytics preferences” in the storefront footer to change or withdraw your choice. Declining optional technologies does not prevent you from shopping. Browser controls can also remove or block cookies, although blocking essential cookies may affect store functions. Checkout and third-party services may provide their own privacy controls.

Usage information can include device and cookie identifiers, IP address, page visits and product interactions. Our custom storefront Google Analytics events do not include your name, email address or phone number. This does not mean that providers receive no technical identifiers or that the same processing applies to every checkout integration.

Learn more from Shopify’s privacy policy, Shopify’s cookie policy, Google’s partner-site notice and Meta’s privacy policy.

Who receives information

We share information as needed with Shopify and other providers supporting hosting, account access, payments, delivery, support and store operations. Analytics and advertising partners receive information according to your choices and applicable law. We may disclose information when you request it, to comply with legal requirements, to protect rights or investigate fraud, or as necessary in a business transfer subject to applicable safeguards. Third-party providers may act under their own privacy notices.

International processing, retention and security

Our providers may store or process information outside the UAE. Applicable transfer requirements and safeguards apply. We retain information for as long as needed for the purposes described here, including fulfilling orders, maintaining accounts, meeting record-keeping obligations and resolving disputes. Retention therefore varies by the type of information and legal requirements.

We use safeguards intended to protect information, but no transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep account access details private and contact us if you suspect unauthorised access.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and its exceptions, you may request access to or a copy of your information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing, or transfer of information. You may withdraw consent for processing that relies on consent. Withdrawal does not change the lawfulness of earlier processing or prevent processing required on another lawful ground.

Contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request and will respond as required by applicable law. You can unsubscribe from promotional emails using their unsubscribe link; necessary order or account messages may still be sent. You may contact us about a privacy complaint and, where applicable, raise it with the competent data protection authority.

Children and external links

Our shopping and membership services are not directed at children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information can contact us. Links to external websites do not make us responsible for their privacy practices; review their notices before sharing information.
